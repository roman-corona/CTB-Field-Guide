Thousands of bins may be stocked out right now

In a two-bin system one open order is normal: one side is being refilled. Two or more open orders on a bin can mean both sides were scanned empty before a refill arrived, which is how BDSI itself defines a stockout. A 98% fill rate allows at most 2% of bins to be stocked out.

What the bin map can't tell us yet

The bin map sets the scope and the fill-rate denominator. Testing the obligations above takes four more pulls.

  • Scan Activity Report. Active vs. inactive bins and every failed scan with its reason.
  • Annual price lists. The Contract column is blank on all 61,518 rows.
  • Order dates. Scan, order and receipt dates to measure the 5-day and 24-hour standards.
  • Bin size history. Min and Max are blank on every row; the resize log shows whether bins were ever reviewed.
